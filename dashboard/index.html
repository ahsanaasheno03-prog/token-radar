Launch Radar

Protocols with no token yet, ranked by what their launch would be worth — and how to farm each one.

The model. Implied value = TVL × peer multiple × growth × seasoning × reach. The peer multiple is the median market-cap / TVL of already-launched protocols in the same category, read from this snapshot — the market prices the guess, not a name list. A category needs three launched peers before its own median prices anything; below that, the global median is used. Every row shows its farm plan's core step, position size and season. Methodology

Implied value is this model's claim, worked out from the snapshot's own figures; it is not a prediction that a token will be issued, or investment advice. Farm plans carry real cost and smart-contract risk — amounts are starting sizes, not recommendations.
